Crown of Embers is a duel of flame spirits. Your life *is* your brazier: a
fire-bowl, and when its flame dies you lose. Every card is a flame spirit with
an ember cost and an attack value — spend embers to play spirits, then send
them at the rival's brazier (or its spirits). Each turn you draw a card and
your embers refill; the rival is a real AI that plays what it can afford and
attacks sensibly. First brazier to 0 loses the crown.

How to play: open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser, or run
`node serve.ts` and open http://localhost:8080. Click a card to select it,
click it again to play it; click one of your spirits on the board, then the
rival brazier or a rival spirit, to attack. Keyboard: 1–7 select a card,
Enter/Space ends your turn, M mutes, R restarts, Esc/P pauses.
